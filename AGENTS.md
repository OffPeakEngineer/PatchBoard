"AI" in this case means a Large Language Model ("LLM"), such as ChatGPT,
Claude, Copilot, Grok, etc.

My brain operates faster than I can type or communicate through traditional
means, in English or otherwise. LLMs have become an accessibility tool for me:
they open channels of expression that were previously blocked by internal
frustrations.

If this repo is publicly accessible, then I have vetted its contents as an
accurate, or functionally safe, demonstration of my own concepts, ideas, and
direction. My use of AI is not safe unless I operate every aspect of that
accessibility tool and understand its operation.
